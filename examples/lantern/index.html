Our pub

Same bar, new stories

The Lantern has stood on the corner of Rope Walk for well over a century: a coaching inn, a dockers' bar, a jazz cellar for one wild summer in 1962. The gas lantern above the door still gets lit every evening.

We took the keys in 2016 and changed as little as possible. The fire still roars, the floor still creaks, and the snug is still where everyone wants to sit. What we added: better beer, a proper little stage and a kitchen that does a serious Sunday roast.

Good to know

Before you pop in

Most gigs are free and first come, first served. For ticketed nights (marked with a price) you can buy at the bar or reserve below.

Every Tuesday at 20:00. Teams of up to six, €2 a head, all of it goes to the winners' bar tab. Tables go fast, so book one if you can.

Kitchen open 12:00 to 21:00 daily: burgers, pies, sharing boards and a famous Sunday roast. Platters for parties can be ordered in advance.

Dogs always. Children until 20:00 with an adult, and anytime in the beer garden in summer.

No room hire if you spend €300 or more on food and drinks. We take a 20% deposit to hold the date, which comes off your bill.
