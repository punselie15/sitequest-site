About us

Gardeners first, designers second

Greenleaf began in 2014 when Anouk swapped an office job for her grandfather's spade. Today we're a team of nine gardeners and one designer, all trained horticulturists who know which plant will thrive in which corner of Ashby Green's clay soil.

We garden the way we'd want our own gardens looked after: peat-free, no unnecessary chemicals, plenty of room for bees, and the same gardener at your gate every visit.
