Our story

A garden shed that got out of hand

Wild Stem began with one flower bed and a market stall. Ten years on, Isla and her small team run a workshop on Mill Lane, buying from a handful of growers nearby and composting every stem we don’t use.

We don’t do identical bouquets. We do what’s at its best this week, arranged loosely, the way flowers grow.
