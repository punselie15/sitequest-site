    Our story

    Sixty years behind the same counter

    Grandad Arthur opened Hartwell’s in 1962 with one block, one knife and a handshake deal with the farm up the lane. We still buy from that farm. Today Tom and his sister Ellen run the shop, and Ellen’s deli counter has grown into a little cheese and pie shop of its own.

    We buy whole animals, so nothing goes to waste and we can cut anything you like. If you’re not sure what to cook, ask: we’ll tell you exactly how long and how hot.

    “If you wouldn’t feed it to your own family, it doesn’t go in the window.”
    Tom Hartwell, third generation
