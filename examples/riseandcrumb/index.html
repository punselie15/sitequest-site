The baker's story

A starter called Doris and a very early alarm

Hi, I'm Nell. I started baking bread in a tiny flat kitchen with a sourdough starter a friend gave me in a jam jar. Her name is Doris, she is now sixteen years old, and she still goes into every loaf we sell.

Rise & Crumb opened on Baker's Row in 2009 with one deck oven and a folding table. Today there are five of us, and we still do it the slow way: 36-hour fermented dough, butter from the dairy up the road, flour stone-milled an hour from here.

Good to know

Little questions

Anything else? Ask at the counter or send us a message below.

48 hours for our standard cakes, and two weeks for tiered or wedding cakes. For something on short notice, call us: we often have a spare.

There is always a vegan loaf and a vegan bake on the counter. Gluten-free cake sponges can be ordered, but our kitchen handles flour all day, so we can't promise no traces.

Tap “Hold one” on any bake that is still available and we keep it behind the till under your name until 16:00. Pay when you pick it up.

Celebration cakes can be delivered within 8 km for €8. Bread is pickup only, so it reaches you as fresh as possible.

Anything left at closing goes to the community fridge on the corner, or becomes tomorrow's bread pudding.
