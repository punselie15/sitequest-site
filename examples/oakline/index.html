06 · About Oakline

A family firm that still sweeps up

Pieter started Oakline in 2003 with a van, a mitre saw and a habit of measuring everything twice. Twenty-two years on we're a crew of eighteen: bricklayers, carpenters, roofers and painters who've mostly worked together for over a decade.

We take on about fifteen projects a year, so the person who quotes your job is the person who runs it. Our own joinery workshop on Mill Yard makes the staircases, windows and kitchens you won't find in a catalogue.

Pieter de Wolde, founder
