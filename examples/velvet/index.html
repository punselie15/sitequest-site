Our studio

Small studio, big care

Anouk opened Velvet in a former flower shop after a decade of working in busy chains where every manicure was a race. Here, there are three tables, soft music, fresh tea and no one watching the clock.

We use hospital-grade sterilised tools, vegan and 12-free polishes, and good ventilation. Your hands (and your lungs) will thank you.
