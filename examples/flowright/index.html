About us

A local team, not a call centre

FlowRight started in 2013 with one van and one rule: turn up when we say, explain the problem in plain words, and leave the place cleaner than we found it. Today we're 14 people, plumbers and electricians under one roof, so one call sorts the leak and the lights.

Every engineer is employed by us (no subcontractors), background-checked, and carries shoe covers, dust sheets and a card reader.
