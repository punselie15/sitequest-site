Our story

A salon that listens first

Maren opened Lumen in 2014 in a former bookbinder's workshop with tall windows and terrible heating. The heating is fixed; the light stayed. It's why we colour by daylight and why we take time over the consultation before anyone picks up scissors.

We use vegan, ammonia-free colour, recycle our foils and hair clippings, and never upsell products you won't use.

"Good hair should look like you, on a good day, every day."
Daylight colourShades matched by the window, not the lamp.
Vegan productsAmmonia-free colour and care.
