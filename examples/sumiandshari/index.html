物語Our story

Ink, rice and patience

Sumi is ink. Shari is sushi rice. Chef Aiko trained as a calligrapher before she ever held a knife, and she still makes sushi the way she paints: one confident stroke, no going back.

The rice is seasoned with a blend of red and white vinegar and served at body temperature. The fish comes in fresh six mornings a week. The conveyor is there for fun. The counter is there for the full story.
