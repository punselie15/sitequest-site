Insurance & costs

Clear about costs, before you come in.

We work with all major health insurers. Not insured for physio? Our prices are fixed and shown upfront.

Covered by most plans

Physio is reimbursed by most supplementary policies, usually 9 to 18 sessions a year. We check your plan for free when you book.

We bill your insurer

With most insurers we send the invoice directly, so you never pay upfront. For massage you get a receipt you can claim yourself.

No referral needed

Come straight to us. If we think you need a doctor or a scan, we’ll tell you and write to your GP for you.
