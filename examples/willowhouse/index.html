Welcome

A house that asks you to stay a little longer

“We bought The Willow House as a ruin in 2014 and spent three years bringing it back to life, one window frame at a time.”

Today Clara and James welcome guests to five rooms filled with old wood, crisp linen and books you’ll want to borrow. There’s a log fire in the snug, a honesty bar in the hall and an acre of garden that runs down to the brook.
