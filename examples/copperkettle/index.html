    Our story

    It started with a dented kettle and a borrowed oven.

    We opened in 2014 in what used to be the corner ironmonger's. The old copper kettle on the shelf came with the building, and we never had the heart to take it down.

    Ten years on we still do things the slow way: beans from a small roaster three streets away, milk from a farm just outside town, and every cake baked here before the doors open. No laptops-only rule, no rush. Stay as long as you like.

    Hannah & Tom, owners
