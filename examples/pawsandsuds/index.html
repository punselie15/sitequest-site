🐾 About us

Groomers who are dog people first

Paws & Suds started when Maya couldn’t find a groomer who’d take her anxious rescue, Pickle, without a cage. So she trained, opened a tiny salon on Station Road, and never looked back.

We book one dog at a time per groomer, so nobody waits in a kennel and nervous pups get all the time they need.
