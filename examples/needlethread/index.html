Our story

Three generations, one workshop.

Rosa Bellini opened Needle & Thread in 1987 with one sewing machine and a rail of other people’s trousers. Her son Marco learned to cut suits at her table. Today Marco and his daughter Giulia run the shop, with Rosa still popping in to check the hems.

We fix what others won’t, explain what we’re doing, and never charge for a job we can’t do well. That’s it. That’s the whole secret.
