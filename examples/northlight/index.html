About

Quiet eye, warm heart.

I started Northlight in a converted boat shed by the harbour, where north-facing windows give the softest light in town. These days I’m mostly out on location, but the studio is still where portraits happen.

I don’t do stiff poses or long group lists. I give a little direction, then step back and watch for the moments in between.

Good to know

Weddings in summer usually book 12 to 18 months ahead. Portrait, family and business sessions are often possible within two to three weeks.

You get every good photo, carefully edited, in full resolution. I leave out blinks and near-duplicates so your gallery stays a joy to look through.

Some of my favourite photos were taken in the rain. I bring clear umbrellas. For sessions, we can also move to the studio or reschedule for free.

Yes. Prints, frames and linen albums can be ordered straight from your gallery and are made by a small print lab I’ve used for years.
