The chef's story

A grandmother's kitchen, a market at dawn

Julien grew up above his grandmother's bistro in a small town in the south, peeling potatoes on an upturned crate. After fifteen years in other people's kitchens, he opened Maison Thyme in 2011 with twelve tables and one rule: cook what is good this week.

“I don't want to surprise you. I want you to remember the taste of a perfect tomato.”

He still walks to the market every Tuesday and Friday, and writes the menu on the way back. Most of the vegetables come from three farms within 30 kilometres; the fish arrives the morning it is landed.
