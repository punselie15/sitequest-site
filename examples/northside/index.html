    04 / THE CREW

    Old shop. New tricks.

    Northside started in 2011 with one chair, one second-hand clipper and a radio stuck on the same station. Thirteen years later there are four chairs, a coffee machine that's never off and a crew that trains together every Monday.

    We're a barbershop for everyone: first cut, fortieth cut, school photos or wedding day. Sit down, switch off, walk out sharp.
